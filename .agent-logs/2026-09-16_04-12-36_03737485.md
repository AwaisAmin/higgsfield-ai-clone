## Why there are two database URLs

You said use the pooled string, and runtime does. But `prisma migrate` takes Postgres advisory locks, which PgBouncer's transaction pooling doesn't support — migrations against the pooled endpoint hang or fail. So pooled drives request-time queries through the Neon adapter, direct drives migrations via `prisma.config.ts`. Flagging it since it's an addition to your spec.
